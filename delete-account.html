Mundane Account & Data Deletion

We respect your privacy and provide complete authority over your personal gameplay records. You can delete your account and all associated cloud data using either method below.

⚠️ Important for Active Installs: If the Mundane app is still installed on your device, please wipe your local data inside the app first (Settings → Danger Zone → Delete Account & Wipe Progress). This ensures your device's local offline cache is completely purged so it cannot re-sync or recreate a new cloud profile on your next launch.
⚡ Option 1: In-App Deletion (Recommended)

If you have Mundane installed, in-app deletion instantly purges your local storage, deletes all synced Firestore cloud records, and revokes your authentication profile:

  1. Open the Mundane app on your Android device.
  2. Tap the Settings (Gear) icon in the sidebar or top bar.
  3. Scroll down to the Danger Zone section.
  4. Tap "Delete Account & Wipe Progress".
  5. Confirm the deletion prompt.

This completely removes both your on-device storage and cloud backups in real time.
