Mundane Account & Data Deletion

We respect your right to control and permanently delete your data. You can delete your account and all associated gameplay information through the mobile app or via this web page.

⚡ Option 1: Immediate In-App Deletion (Recommended)

If you still have the Mundane app installed on your device, you can instantly and irreversibly delete all local and cloud data yourself:

  1. Open the Mundane app.
  2. Tap the Settings (Gear) icon in the top right.
  3. Scroll down to the Danger Zone section.
  4. Tap "Delete Account & Wipe Progress".
  5. Confirm the deletion prompt.

This immediately wipes your local storage, deletes your synced Firestore profile, and deletes your Firebase user account.

🌐 Option 2: Web Deletion Request (App Uninstalled)

If you uninstalled Mundane and want your synced cloud backup permanently purged from Google Firebase Firestore, enter the Google Account email you used to sign in:

Request Submitted! Opening your email client to verify the request. Once verified, your cloud profile will be purged within 30 days.
ℹ️ What Data Is Deleted?
  • Character Profile: Player nickname, level, stats, and gold currency.
  • Companions & Inventory: Partner rosters, acquired spells, items, and artifacts.
  • Task History & Chronicles: Completed task titles, streaks, and narrative event logs.
  • Authentication Record: Your Firebase Auth UID is permanently revoked.

Retention Policy: Local device data is destroyed immediately upon in-app reset or app uninstallation. Cloud records requested via web are verified and purged within 30 calendar days per GDPR/CCPA standards.
