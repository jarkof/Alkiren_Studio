2. Summary: Privacy by Design

  • Local by Default: Your daily habits, checklists, task completions, and character progress are stored locally on your device via encrypted local storage.
  • No Data Brokering: We never sell, monetize, rent, or trade your personal information or gameplay records to third-party data brokers or advertising networks.
  • No Ads: Mundane contains zero third-party behavioral advertising banners, interstitials, or tracking cookies.
  • Voice Privacy: Speech-to-text dictation for drafting quests occurs strictly on-device using Android speech recognition. No audio recordings are ever stored on our servers or transmitted to remote cloud databases.

3. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide

  • Gameplay & Task Content: Custom task titles, heroic quest descriptions, subtask checklists, and journal ledger entries you write. For standard/free users, these stay strictly on your local device.
  • Character Profile: Hero nickname, chosen companion name, and leveling statistics.

B. Pro Tier & Cloud Synchronization Data (Optional)

If you subscribe to Mundane Pro or link your account with Google Sign-In, the App securely backs up your gameplay data to Google Firebase Firestore. This cloud profile includes:

  • Your unique Firebase / Google User ID (a cryptographic alphanumeric string).
  • Your Google Account email address (used strictly for authentication).
  • Game progression data (companion roster, level, gold, streaks, unlocked achievements).

C. Device Permissions

  • Microphone (Audio): Used solely to enable live speech-to-text dictation for drafting quests. Dictation is transcribed in real time. We do not record, store, or transmit your voice files.
  • Notifications: Used solely to deliver local daily quest alerts and reminder notifications scheduled by you. You can disable notifications anytime in the App or Android system settings.

D. Anonymous Diagnostics & Stability Telemetry

We use Firebase Crashlytics to identify bugs, crashes, and performance bottlenecks. Collected data includes anonymous crash stack traces, device model, operating system version, and general error logs. You can disable telemetry at any time directly in the App under Settings > Privacy & Legal > Telemetry.

4. Artificial Intelligence & Third-Party Services

Mundane uses select third-party services to deliver core gameplay features:

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC): Cloud database (Firestore), user authentication, and crash analytics. Governed by Firebase Privacy & Security.
  • Google Gemini / Vertex AI: Used to generate dynamic RPG quest narratives, Daily Chronicle story recaps, and tavern dialogue. Important: Prompts sent to Vertex AI contain only generic game context (e.g., companion name, fantasy task summary). No personally identifying information (PII) is ever transmitted, and your personal data is never used to train public AI foundation models.
  • Google Play Billing (Google LLC): In-app purchases and subscriptions are handled entirely by Google Play. Alkiren Studio never receives, processes, or stores your credit card numbers or financial account credentials.

5. Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR / UK GDPR)

If you reside in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, our processing of your personal data is governed by the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a Contract: Synchronizing your character progress and providing Pro features upon your request.
  • Legitimate Interests: Monitoring crash telemetry to maintain app stability, prevent fraud, and optimize game performance.
  • Consent: When you grant Android runtime permissions (e.g., Microphone or Notifications) or opt in to telemetry.

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion

  • Local Data: You can wipe all local records anytime by tapping Settings > Danger Zone > Wipe Progress & Reset Game or uninstalling the App.
  • Cloud Data Deletion: You have the absolute right to delete all synced cloud data at any time:
    1. In-App: Go to Settings > Danger Zone > Delete Account. This permanently deletes your cloud record and resets local data.
    2. Web Portal: If you uninstalled the App, visit our dedicated self-service deletion portal at https://mundane-76d8e.web.app/delete-account.html or email us at alkiren44@gmail.com. All cloud records will be purged within 30 days.

7. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)

Mundane is designed for general audiences aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 (or 16 in certain EU jurisdictions). If we discover that personal data from a child under 13 has been collected without verifiable parental consent, we will promptly delete that data.

8. Your Rights (EEA, UK, California & Global)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • The right to access and receive a copy of your personal data.
  • The right to rectify inaccurate or incomplete records.
  • The right to erasure ("Right to be Forgotten").
  • The right to restrict or object to data processing.
  • The right to data portability.
  • The right to withdraw consent at any time without affecting prior lawful processing.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us directly at alkiren44@gmail.com. We respond to all verified requests within 30 days.
