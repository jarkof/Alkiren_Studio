⚔️ Why I Built Mundane

Mundane started from a simple personal frustration: everyday productivity apps and habit trackers felt like boring homework. Checking off a task shouldn't feel like filling out a spreadsheet—it should feel like completing a meaningful quest in an unfolding fantasy adventure.

I wanted a game with genuine RPG depth: companion creatures you actually bond with and train, dynamic journal chronicles written about your day's achievements, a living tavern with quirky characters, and tactical loot encounters. Most importantly, I wanted it built with respect for player privacy—local-first data storage and zero intrusive advertisements.

📦 Legacy Architecture Tools

Earlier in my journey, I built open-source C# frameworks for Unity game development (like the Code-First FSM, LevelUp Engine, and Ability System) to solve code-architecture headaches. While those libraries remain freely available, my full active focus and passion are now poured into building Mundane.
